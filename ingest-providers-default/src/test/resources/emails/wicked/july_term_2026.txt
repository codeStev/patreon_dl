THE FEATURES

The Clint Barton Ronin Sculpture comes with 2 portrait options, giving you two different ways to bring this darker version of Clint to the shelf and choose the face that fits your display best.

The Fight Club Diorama comes with a simple base, keeping all the attention on Tyler and Edward and the incredible confrontation between them. We designed these two to belong together because, come on,  separating these dudes just doesn’t feel right!!!!

But yep, Tyler and Edward are also included as individual sculptures, each with a simplified base for those who prefer to display them separately. Still, our recommendation stands: print both!!!!

And to complete the Movies loot, both characters also arrive as individual Portrait Busts.
